# ADR-009 — Independent brand and anonymized evidence

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-07-15
- Decision: D-009
- Direct tasks: UI-002, DOC-002

## Context

The project has been separated from the original company context. Continued use of company branding, real identities or data in a public thesis/demo is not assumed to be authorized.

## Decision

Rebrand the application as independent “GTAS VPP”. Remove or replace company names, logos and assets, and anonymize seed data, screenshots, exports, logs and thesis evidence before final delivery.

## Consequences and guardrails

- Rebranding does not change business behavior merely to match the thesis.
- Final repository/evidence sweeps must cover personal data and proprietary branding, not just visible shell text.
- A later written authorization would require a new decision; it does not retroactively make old evidence safe.
